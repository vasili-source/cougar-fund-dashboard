Overview Portfolio Performance Risk Valuation Methodology

Methodology & Assumptions

Data Sources

  • SEC EDGAR XBRL company facts
  • Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED)

Valuation Framework

  • Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)
  • Free Cash Flow derived from SEC filings
  • Terminal value via Gordon Growth Model

Key Assumptions

  • WACC assumed constant within sensitivity bounds
  • Terminal growth rate < discount rate
  • No forward-looking forecasts beyond filings

Limitations

  • Educational use only
  • No investment advice
  • Intrinsic values are estimates, not prices
